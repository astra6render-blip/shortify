FAQ

Questions, short answers

Do my videos get uploaded anywhere?

No. This build works entirely on the link: metadata and storyboards are fetched on demand, and editing stays in your browser. Nothing is stored on our side.

Is this the real YouTube auto-cutter?

This is the full product experience — paste, auto-cut proposals, and a working editor. Downloads and server-side rendering are stubbed with a clear demo mode so you can try every effect safely.

What formats can I export?

The studio composes 9:16, 1:1 and 4:5 frames at 1080p. The export panel assembles your cut plan (clips, effects, captions) and renders it to MP4 through the bundled server when you run Shortify locally.

Does it cost anything?

Shortify is open and free to run. Feed it links to content you own or that's licensed for reuse — the demo reel is Big Buck Bunny, released by Blender under CC-BY.
