One-click YouTube connect

Grab your full cookie jar from the YouTube tab — including the HttpOnly sign-in cookies that document.cookie can't see.

checking…
1

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar

If the bar is hidden: Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+B

Send cookies to Shortify

No bookmarks bar (phone/tablet)? Use the manual box in step 3 instead.

2

Open YouTube, sign in, click the bookmark

  1. Open youtube.com in the same browser and sign in.
  2. Click the Send cookies to Shortify bookmark you just saved.
  3. It sends your cookie jar to this page and returns here.

Nothing is stored on YouTube. The bookmark only reads cookies from your own browser and posts them to your own Shortify server.

3

Or paste a cookies.txt file

Install the extension “Get cookies.txt LOCALLY”, export from youtube.com, open the file and paste everything here.
